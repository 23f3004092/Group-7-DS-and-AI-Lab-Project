Modalities to be adopted by states and the supplying agencies for minikits distribution as per
new guidelines of NFNSM for FY 2025-26:
The Terms and Conditions for Minikits distribution is applicable as per the new operational guidelines
of National Food Security & Nutrition Mission (NFSNM) 2025-26 given at point No. 2 (C) (i) to 2 (C) (xvi)
in page No, 26 lo 29 of new guidelines. ln addition, the competent authority has approved following
conditions:
1) The Seed lnventory Module developed by Seeds Division of DA&FW is to be used for recording and
supply of receipt from State agencies. The same online system be used to generate the
acknowledgement slip which will be duly signed and stamped by competent authority not below the
rank of Director (Agriculture)/Mission Director (NFSNM),
2) Further, along with the copies of acknowledgement receipts from the districts, an original
consolidated acknowledgement with the approval of Principal Secretary (Agri,)/Secretary (Agri,) of the
respective states in the following format may be furnished to this Department.
Name of the No. ol minikits Supply No. ol bio-tertilirer No. of leaflets
Sl. No. Crop Variety Lot No.
District supplied Date packets supplied supplied
Note: Reimbursement of claims will be entertained as per the consolidated acknowledgement furnished by the
agencies as per above table.
3) There will be POS based distribution by States in the areas/clusters already identified under
Pulse mission for those who are taking pulses for the first time. For others, the distribution at 50 %
subsidy will be undertaken for new varieties as provided in mission.4) A farmer who has availed a
minikit under this component will be ineligible to receive another minikit for the same crop or variety for
a minimum period of two years.
5) The details of farmers (Farmer lD and if Farmer lD not there then land details including Khasra No.,
LGD code of village) be recorded and the same shall be cross verified by DCS data to confirm that
same crop was grown by the farmer to which minikits were supplied.
6) The reimbursement claim by State Seed Agencies/Central Seed Agencies for minikit distribution
should be submitted within 6 months of supply (cut-off date), else reasons of delay be furnished. The
reimbursement claims with the delay in reasons for late submission of claims will be entertained by this
Department untrll 2 years from the date of administrative approval failing in which the agencies will lose
their claim amount,
7) Provisions made in the portal for allocation of seed minikits and its further distribution to districts and
beneficiaries/ farmers are to be uploaded in the following NFSM portal time to ti (u
https//NFSM.gov.in/mis/AdmApprovalMinikit.aspx).
