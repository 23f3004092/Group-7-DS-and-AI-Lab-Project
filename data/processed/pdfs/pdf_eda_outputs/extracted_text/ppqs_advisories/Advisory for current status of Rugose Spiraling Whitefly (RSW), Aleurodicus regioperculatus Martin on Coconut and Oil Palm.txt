Pest Management OpfuO
The following management strategies may be adopted for management of Rugose
Spiralling Whitefly (RSW) on Coconut and Oil Palm:
1. Install yellow sticky traps in the orchards to trap adult whiteflies'
Z. Encourage build-up of parasitoid (Encarsfa sp.) in the orchards and re-introduce
parasitized pupae in the whitefly-infested orchards.
3 Conserve and augment predators of RSW such as Chrysoperla and Coccinellids, which
.
are available in the field.
4. Apply I oh starchsolution on leaflets to flake out the sooty moulds.
5. In severe cases, spray only neem oil 0.5% or NSKE 5Yo and avoid spraying any form of
insecticides.
6 Entomopathogenic fungus Isarifumosoroseq has been found to be effective in managing
RSW, hence Processing companies, SAUs, ICAR institutes, KVKs, CIPMCs and State
Agricultural Departments of respective states should mass multiply it in large scale for
making available to the needy farmers.
7 Regular training programme for the extension workers and farmers should be organized
by SAUs/ICAR institutes/ClPMCs/ KVKs for up-gradation of their knowledge and skill
regarding management of RSW'
8. Regular survey and surveillance should be conducted for monitoring the incidence of RSW'
g. Sensitization and awareness programmes should be conducted to farmers and other
stakeholders regarding IPM management strategies of RSW.
10. Assessment ofthe population ofRSW should be worked out and extent of damage should
be determined to advice farmers accordingly for the effective management measures'
In view of the above, it is advised that the affected areas of Tumkur, Mandya, Mysore and
Ramanagara district of Karnataka, East and West Godavari of Andhra Pradesh and also other
states where incidence of this pest was reported last year as Srikakulam, Vizianagatam and
Vishakhapatnam districts of Andhra Pradesh, Mysore and Mandya districts of Karnataka,
pallathur, Karambakudi and Pattukottai villages in Thanjavur District of Tamil Nadu should be
monitored for RSW in Coconut and Oil Palm orchards on regular basis and if more incidence of
Rugose Spiraling Whitefly is noticed bythe farmers, extension functionaries and even through the
newspapers or other source, survey of the affected area may be conducted immediately and the
