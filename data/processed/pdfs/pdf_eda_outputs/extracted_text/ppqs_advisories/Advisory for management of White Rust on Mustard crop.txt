sub: Advisory for management of white Rust on Mustard crop -reg.
Sir,
it is to bring to your notice that incidence of White Rust caused by Atbugo candida on
Mustard crop is reported in Sirsa and Fatehabad districts of Haryana. In Fatehabad district, in
2112.5 acres out of 60925 acres of total area of mustard crop i.e., up to 25Yo damage was
observed.
The symptoms of disease appears as white to creamy yellow pustules on the lower surface
of leaves. The pustules, later coalesce to form patches on the lower surface of leaves.
Moist (more than7}%o relative humidity) coupled with warm weather and intermittent rains
favours disease development. The fungus is likely to damage mustard crop, which has potential
to bring down the average yield by 30-40% in late sowrr crops. It is at the initial stage and
unlikely to cause major damage if farmers take timely measures.
Keeping in view the current crop stage and prevailing weather condition, disease may
spread further. Important IPM for management of White Rust are listed below
1. Removal and complete destruction of diseased infested plant parts by buming so that
the further spread of diseases is reduced.
2.
Clean cultivation helps to avoid disease incidence.
3.
Get rid of the weeds in and around the field.
4.
Fungicides should be used as per label claim approved by the registration committee.
