Sub:- Advisory on Late Blight disease of Potato-reg.
Late Blight is a conlmon disease of Potato caused by the fungus Phytophthora
infestans. The disease affects all plant parts and causes characteristic leaf spots and blight. Low
temperature together with saturated atmosphere is congenial for development of Late Blight
disease. In view of the prevailing congenial weather conditions, the chances of disease
development is high and pest management interventions are imperative to check the possible
development of the disease.
The following practices of IPM strategies may be followed for management of Late
Blight disease in Potato crop:
o
Collect and destroy infested crop debris.
o
Maintain field sanitation.
o
A strong and foolproof quality control system for fertilizers, bio-fertilizers, fungicides
needs to be enforced.
