Advisory on incidence of Falt Army worm (FAw) on cotton crop in Maharashtra state - regarding.
5lr.
I have the honour to bring to your kind notice that as per the letter received from ICAR-Central Institute for
{ Cotton Research, Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR), Nagpur, Maharashtra vide F.No. PS 1(FAWy2O19-20
dated 2Jtt'September, 2019 and,letter from cotton Improvement proiect. Mahatma
Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth, Rahuri vide Out No. Cotton/83312019 dated 20.09.2019, FAW has been reported for the first
time in India on Cotton.
This is the first record of FAW on Cotton in India invaded from infeste d. maize crop, so it is necessary to be
alert about the infestation of FAW in Maharashtra and create awareness in the adjoining states: Gujarat, Andhra pradesh,
Punjab, Karnataka and Telangana state. The following IPM Strategies should be followed for effective manasement of
the pest.
a Integratecl Pest management needs to be promoted by involving impor-tant pest management tools.
a Monitoring and surveillance on regular basis and following IpM practices.
a The inf-ested flowers and green bolls of cotton should be prornptly removed and destroyed.
a Timely management at early instar level by hand collection of egg masses carrying leaves and destroying them with
larvae.
Trichog"amma parasitoids may be released directly or Tricho-card can be pinned to the leaves of standing cotton
plants in the fields for destroying the egg stage.
Use of neem based pesticide at initial stage of the pest for augmentation and conservation of potential parasitoids
and predators.
a Infested crop residues should be removed and destructed promptly.
a Use glossy lure pheromone baited traps for the control of adult male moths.
a Farmers must be advised to maintain field sanitation at the vicinity of the main field and nearby areas must be kept
free liom weeds.
Regular training programmes for the extension workers/ pesticide dealers and farmers should be organized for
upgradation of knowledge and skill.
a Pesticides must be used as per label claim approved by the registration committee.
a IPM package of practices for cotton need to be promoted the IPM package of practices for cotton can be
downloaded from http://ppqs.gov.in/sites/default/flles/cotton.pdf or from http:llfarmers.gov.in.
Regular meetings of State Pest Surveillance and advisory unit and District pest Surveillance and Advisory Unit may
be conducted.
As recommended from CICR, Nagpur, entomopathogenic fungi Metarhizium anisopliae @ 59 or Nomuraea rileyi
@ 59 per litre of water may be sprayed on cotton crop.
As recornmended from CICR, Nagpur, Spinetoram 1 1.7 SC @ 0.8 ml or Chlorantraniliprole l g.5 SC @ 0.3 ml per
litre of water can be sprayed to already infested cotton crop to avoid further damage. Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG
& 1'g%EC (approved pesticides for Pink Boll Worm (PBw) on Cotton) may also be used for the manasement of
FAW in cotton.
The hot spot of FAW should be identified for survey and teams may be constituted for regular surveillance and
monitoring of FAW situation. Need based plant protection measures should be adopted to avoid economic loss to the
cotton crop. Tirnely supply of qLralitv pesticides / bio-pesticides for the control of FAW on Cotton should be ensured.
In view of the above, it is requested to monitor the cotton crop on regular basis and if major incidence of FAW
is repofted by the iarmers, extension functionaries and even through newspapers or other sources, survey ofthe affected
may be conducted immediately and the concerned officials at the district/ division/ block may be advised to take the
suitable plant protection measures immediately. The detailed information may also be sent to the Directorate throueh
email ppa@nic.in urgently to apprise the same to Depafiment of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers welfare.
