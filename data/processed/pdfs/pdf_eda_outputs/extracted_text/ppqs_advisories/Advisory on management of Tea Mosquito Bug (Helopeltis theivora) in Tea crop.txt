Sub: Advisory on management of Tea Mosquito Bug (Helopeltis theivora) in Tea crop -reg.
Sir,
This is to bring to your notice that the incidence of Helopeltis theivora (Tea Mosquito Bug) has
reported above moderate intensity from Tea growing districts of Tamil Nadu state. It is a serious pest
and may cause damage & eventually yield loss. Considering its status and host range which includes
cashew, cinchona, cocoa, neem, cotton, cowpea, red gram, henna, apple, grapes, guava, avocado,
drumstick, mango, ber, Annona reticulata, allspice, black pepper, Ailanthus, mahogany, annatto,
Thespesia populnea, Solanum nigrum, Singapore cherry, ornamental Acalypha etc. and keeping in
view the current crop stage and prevailing weather conditions, population of this pest is expected to
build up. Therefore, Tea and other host crop should be monitored regularly for pest build up and
adoption of Integrated Pest Management techniques. Agro Ecosystem Analysis (AESA) based IPM
Package of practices of Tea is available at DPPQ&S website in the link http://ppqs.gov.in/ipm-
packages. The Important IPM practices for management of Tea Mosquito Bug are listed below:
o
Regular survey and monitoring its population from initiation of flushing till fruit set, is crucial
to initiate timely management measures.

and Lantana camara offer excellent hiding places and serve as alternate host for the tea
mosquito bug. Growth of weeds and wild host plants near in and around tea fiel{ may be
controlled and this will help to reduce the growth of tea mosquito bug population.
