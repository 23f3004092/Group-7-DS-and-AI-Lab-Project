on Potato crop has been reported at Low to Moderate level in Agra, Mathura and Hathras
districts of Uttar Pradesh state. Its infestation may further spread in other Potato growing areas
of state if favourable weather conditions prevail.
Keeping in view of the severity of the disease, it is advised that the all Potato growing
areas of Uttar Pradesh state may be intensively monitored during Rapid Roving Survey for

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) at village level need to be developed.

Blight disease in Potato crop
