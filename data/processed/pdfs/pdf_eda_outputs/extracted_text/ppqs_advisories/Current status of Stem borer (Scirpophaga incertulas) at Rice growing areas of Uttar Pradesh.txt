Sub: Current status of Stem borer (Scirpophuga incertulas) at Rice growing areas of
Uttar Pradesh-Advisory reg.
Sir,
I have the honourlo bring your kind notice that incidence of stem borer (Scirpophaga
incertulas) on rice crop has been reported at Low to Moderate level in Nari, Gauhari, Borka
Bokhari, Jab chhoti, Bathan, Bari Bathan, Piparawala and Khadona villages and at Severe level
in Gauhari, Nari and Semali villages of Chhata block of Mathura district of Uttar Pradesh state.
Its incidence may finther spread in other rice growing areas of State if favourable weather
conditions prevail.
Keeping in view of the severity of the pest, it is advised that the all rice growing areas
of Uttar Pradesh state may be intensively monitored during Rapid Roving Survey for
assessment of population of Stem borer. Therefore, strong linkage among State Agriculture
Department, State Agricultural University, ICAR Institutes and farmers to implement
Integrated Pest Management (IPM) at village level need to be developed.
The following practices of IPM strategies may be followed for management of Stem
borer on rice crop
1. Farmers should be educated to identifu, conserve and augment the natural enemies available
in the rice field viz., Parasitoids like Trichogramma spp., Tetrastichus schoenobii,
Telenomus roruani, Amauromorpha accepta, Cotesia Jlavipes, Bracon sp., Xanthopimpla
flavolineata, etc. and predators like Spiders etc.
2. Farmers should be advised through extension functionaries to use only recommended doses
of fertilizers and pesticides as per approved label claims by GIB&RC.
J a . A strong and foolproof quality control system for fertilizers, bio-fertilizers, pesticides & bio
pesticides needs to be enforced.
4. Regular training programme for the extension workers, pesticide dealers and farmers should
organized by the SAUs/ICAR institutes/ClPMCs/ KVKs for up-gradation of their knowledge
and skill regarding management of Stem borer.
5. Insecticides must be used as per label claim approved by the Central Insecticides Board and
RegistrationCommittee,Detailedinformationisavailableat
rclmajor-uses-of-pesticides However, list of recommended pesticides for Stem borer is
attached. (Annexure-I)
6, IPM package of practices for rice need to be promoted, The IPM package of practise for rice
may be downloaded from web -link. http ://ppq s. eov. inlsite s/default/fi les/rice.odf

and if any more incidence of Stem borer is reported by the far-.rs, extension functionaries
