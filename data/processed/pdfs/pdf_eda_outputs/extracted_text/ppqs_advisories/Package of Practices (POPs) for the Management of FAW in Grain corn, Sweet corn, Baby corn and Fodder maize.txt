^ Preparation of Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) for one acre- 10 kg of need seed kernel is required for
one acre. Grind 10 kg of need seed kernels to make powder. Soak the powder in 50 litres of water overnight. Stir
and filter the contents using cotton cloth. Add 200 g detergent powder or 200 ml of soap solution to the filtered
solution. Make up the volume to 200 litres by adding water.
