Sir,
You are aware that the soybean yellow mosaic is a serious and widespread disease of
soybean and its incidence was observed in Madhya Pradesh & Maharashtra states during last two
years. The disease is caused by Soybean Yellow Mosaic Virus (SYMV), the virus is sap
transmiffed and spread by vector white fly (Bemisia tabaci). The disease symptoms appear as
yellow spots which are either scattered or produced in indefinite bands along the major veins of
soybean leaves. Rusty necrotic spots appear in the yellow areas as the leaves mature. Some time
severe mottling and crinkling of leaves are also seen. Leaves of severely infected plants become
yellow when they are young. Affected plants bear less flower and pods. The infection results in
decrease in oil and increase in protein content.
It is observed that the disease incidence is progressing in soybean growing areas during
past years and it may increase on other soybean growing areas. As the Soybean Yellow Mosaic
Virus is transmitted by insect vector and its incidence is to be observed at early crop stage so
there is requirement for adoption of integrated approach from sowing of the crop.
Keeping in view the ensuing Kharif season, the soybean crop should be monitored
regularly for disease incidence and adopt Integrated Pest Management techniques beginning
from sowing. Important immediate and long term IPM practices for management of Soybean
Yellow Mosaic Virus are listed at Annexure-I
Further, it is informed that the Government of India, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers
Welfare has launched the Al-driven National Pest Surveillance System (NPSS) application
which is designed to enhance pest surveillance and management across the nation, providing

Important immediate and long term IPM practices for management of Soybean
Yellow Mosaic Virus
I.Immediate Measures
1. Regularly monitor population of whitefly and take effective IPM measures to manase its
population.
2.
Use of yellow sticky traps (20-25 traps/ha) in early crop stage to catch the flying adults of
white fly thereby helps to minimizing the further transmission of yMV.
3. Use of CIBRC recommended chemicals at right time: For white fly management in
standing crop, as soon as the symptoms appear, spray the crop with Acetamipii d 25yo +
Bifenthrin 25 % WG @ 250gm in 500 litre of waterlha.If the pest crossed Economic
Threshold level (ETL) i.e., 21 Nymphs or Adults per leaf. The list of CIB & RC
approved pesticides for management of vector of disease (White fly) in soybean is
available on https:i/ppqs.gov.inldivisions/cib-rc/major-uses-of-pesticides
4.
Identification, removal and destruction of infested plants from the held: Plants showing
symptoms of Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV) should be removed from the field to p.errent
further spread of the disease.
5.
Avoid water stagnation in the field during crop stand.
II. Long term measure:
1. Advised to follow recommended sowing time.
2. Use of recommended tolerant/resistant varieties.
3. Use preventive methods (using disease-free seeds, adopting crop rotation, and inter
croppmg).
4.
Seed treatment: Before sowing, treat the seed with Thiamethoxam 30 % WS or
Thiamethoxam 30 %FS @ 6 ml/kg seed
5. Use of recommended seed rate, spacing and use of fertilizers.
6. The weeds in nearby area should be removed/ destroyed as virus survives in them in off
season.
7.
Removal and destruction of infested/ infected plants from the field after harvest of the
crop.
